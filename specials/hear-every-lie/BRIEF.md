# Special: "What If You Could Hear Every Lie?"

One-off film for the Shift Happens channel, separate from the daily series: not an episode, doesn't touch the
story, the votes or the episode numbering. Style: `STYLE_WHATIF_FILM.md`. English, 16:9, about 5 minutes.

## Cast

- **YOU** — 19, night clerk at the Hotel Larkspur. Black silhouette style, red cap, mustard vest, brass name tag.
- **OTIS** — night porter, 71. Gray tufts, burgundy porter's jacket, brass buttons. Lives in staff room 009.
- **MR. VANE** — the owner. Tall, white suit, thin tie.
- **MS. QUILL** — guest in 412. Yellow umbrella indoors, always.
- **MOM** — appears on phone and in one flashback. Green cardigan.
- Extras — guests in hotel robes.

Voices (edge-tts): narrator `en-US-AndrewNeural` (+0%, unhurried), you `en-US-BrianNeural`, otis `en-US-GuyNeural`
(-6%), vane `en-US-ChristopherNeural`, quill `en-GB-SoniaNeural`, mom `en-US-JennyNeural`, guests from the other en-US voices.

The bell: a short, clean reception-bell ding made with ffmpeg. It's the film's signature, so keep it the same
every time. `[DING]` in the script marks it, right at the end of the lie. The speaker's mouth flashes with a thin brass ring.

## Script

Each number is one shot or a short run of shots. Facts: people tell one or two lies a day on average (Serota et al.,
2010); people spot lies about 54% of the time (Bond & DePaulo, 2006).

**Cold open**

1. Top-down: a brass reception bell on a wood desk. A hand slides a room key across.
   GUEST: "Lovely stay. Really." [DING] — nobody touched the bell.
2. Side-on lobby, 3 a.m. You don't look up. GUEST: "And I didn't take the towels." [DING]
   YOU: "Enjoy the towels, sir."

**The what-if**

3. Dark scene: the key rack, 212 keys. NARRATOR: "The average person tells one or two lies a day."
4. The keys light up in a wave. "Most people catch a lie about half the time. Fifty-four percent. Barely better than a coin."
5. Big number on dark, then close-up of you. "You? One hundred. You've got no idea why. Every lie, out loud, in your head. A small, polite bell."
6. Close-up. "So what's it like, hearing every lie? Great. And terrible. Sometimes in the same sentence."

**Perks and costs** (corner key tag: PERK or COST. Warm when PERK, gray-blue when COST.)

7. PERK. Top-down cards. "Nobody beats you at poker." OTIS: "Busted flush? You owe me nine dollars." [no ding] "Otis pays."
8. PERK. Lobby. "Nobody scams the front desk." MAN: "I'm with the fire marshal's office." [DING] "Sir, you're with the vending machine company."
9. PERK. Top-down map of the hotel with pill labels: "CHECKED OUT, HE SAYS", "STILL IN THE ROOM". "You know who's skipping the bill before they reach the elevator."
10. COST, grayscale. Phone call, split-screen. MOM: "Money's fine, sweetie. Don't worry about me." [DING] NARRATOR: "You can't un-hear that."
11. COST. Date at a diner. "This is fun. I'm having fun." [DING] "You pay. You walk home."
12. COST. Staff party, cake. A row of faces. "Love the haircut." [DING] "Great to have you." [DING] "We should hang out." [DING] [DING] "A whole room, ringing."
13. COST. Close-up, the blurred lobby behind you. "Here's what nobody tells you. Polite is mostly lying. Kind is mostly lying. You can't get a compliment you believe."
14. Line-art diagram of a head, with a bell inside, swinging. "So you stopped asking people things. Questions are how you get hurt."
15. You, alone at the desk, hand over a new rule on a sticky note. "And you made one rule. You never lie. Not once. You know what it sounds like."

**Otis**

16. The staff room 009 door, a porter's jacket on a hanger. "And then there's Otis."
17. Top-down: he polishes a brass button with his thumb. "Forty years of carrying other people's bags."
18. Side-on: Otis and you at 4 a.m., two mugs. OTIS: "This coffee's terrible." [no ding] "That's why you like him."
19. Close-up. OTIS: "You're a strange kid." [no ding] "Three years. Not one bell. He's the only person in your life who has never rung."
20. Close-up, you nearly smile. OTIS: "But you're all right." [no ding] "You've never been so glad to hear nothing."

**Room 412**

21. Rain, revolving door. A yellow umbrella stays open indoors. QUILL: "One night. Room four twelve. Business." "Nothing unusual. You hand her the key."
22. Next shot, daytime gray. Mr. Vane with an inspector. VANE: "The alarms? Tested this morning." [DING] Your head turns.
23. Close-up of Vane: "Fully up to code." [DING] [DING]
24. Basement, flashlight. Full-screen panel: ALARM SYSTEM — BYPASSED. Date from six weeks ago. "It's cheaper not to fix it. And nobody ever asks."
25. Vane's office door. YOU: "The alarm's off." VANE: "Don't be silly. I'd never put my guests at risk." [DING] "You've still got a job, kid. Do you want to keep it?" [no ding]

**The fire**

26. 3:12 a.m. Big number on dark, then the laundry room: a dryer, an orange glow under the door.
27. Torn split: smoke on the stairs / you at the desk, hitting the alarm button. Nothing. Silence.
28. You call it in. Full-screen phone: DISPATCH — "NINE MINUTES". "212 guests. Nine minutes. No alarm."
29. You run floors, banging on doors. GUEST in robe: "Is this a drill?" YOU: "It's a real fire!" GUEST: "Right. Sure, kid." The door shuts. Another: "Call the front desk." YOU: "I AM the front desk."
30. Map of the hotel from above: only three dots moving. A clock counts. "You've told the truth your whole life. Nobody's listening."
31. Otis, at the top of the stairs with a master key. OTIS: "You've got the PA. So talk to 'em in a language they understand."
32. Close-up on you at the microphone. Slow push. The sticky note with your rule, in the corner of the frame. Silence for two full seconds.
33. YOU, on every speaker: "Good morning, guests! Free breakfast on the front lawn, in the next three minutes only. First come. Pancakes." The biggest [DING] of the film, ringing through every floor.
34. Comic pop: doors bursting open. Robes everywhere. GUEST: "Did he say pancakes?!" Map from above: 212 dots pour out of the building.
35. Fire trucks, flashing light. "Two hundred and twelve people, in robes, on a lawn. Furious, and alive."

**Payoff**

36. Crowd count at dawn light. "Two hundred and twelve. Every guest." Close-up of you, looking around the lawn. "Then you count one more. Otis went back up, to check the third floor."
37. Firefighters carry him out on a stretcher. You run alongside. Oxygen mask, soot on the buttons.
38. Close-up. OTIS, weak: "I'm not scared." [DING] Your face. "Three years. The first one."
39. You take his hand. YOU: "You're going to be fine." [DING] It's your second lie today, and the bell is yours too.
40. He laughs under the mask. OTIS: "You're a terrible liar." [no ding] Ambulance doors. Hold on the sound of nothing.
41. Morning. News graphic: HOTEL OWNER "HAD NO IDEA". Vane on camera: "I had no idea." [DING] You hand the inspector the panel log.
42. Hospital, warm light. Otis is sitting up, polishing a button, complaining about the coffee. OTIS: "This coffee's terrible." [no ding] "You laugh. Right on time."
43. Your phone. MOM: "Money's fine, sweetie." [DING] YOU: "I know, Mom. I love you too." "Some bells, you just let ring."

**The twist**

44. The curb outside the burned hotel. Yellow umbrella, in full sun. QUILL: "Clever. The pancakes."
45. QUILL: "I'm a dentist from Ohio." Silence. "I was asleep during the fire." Silence. "I've never told a lie in my life." Silence.
46. Slow push on your face. "No bell. Not one. You've never heard that much silence."
47. She sets a key on the hood of a fire truck, with a 412 tag. QUILL: "Your hearing is very good. Mine's better. Check-out's at noon."

**End card**

48. Dark. Big type: WOULD YOU WANT TO HEAR EVERY LIE? Cast in a circle of warm light: you, Otis, a yellow umbrella.
    NARRATOR: "Would you want it? Comment one word. Bell, or silence."
