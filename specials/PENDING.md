# Pending for the next "what if" films

Published so far: "What If You Could Hear Every Lie?" (v2, public), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ho04g8eKXVg
The relit v2 got much closer to the reference, but these differences remain. Fix them before or during the next film.

## Still different from the reference

1. **Character animation.** Ours mostly talk, change expression and walk, while the bodies stay still. The reference has
   more gestures, head turns and small actions. This is the biggest gap.
2. **Set detail.** Our rooms have fewer objects and less texture. The reference is full of small things: magnets,
   calendars, dish racks, tablecloth patterns, stitching.
3. **Voice and music.** Free edge-tts voices and a synthesized bed. Nobody on the production side has judged them by ear,
   and emotional lines may sound cold.
4. **Rack focus.** Described in `STYLE_WHATIF_FILM.md` but not used in the film yet.
5. **Top-down shots.** The reference draws the hands, phones and objects larger and with more detail.
